## Why the It's Fine Demo Is Worth Your Time

There is a special kind of joy in a game that turns a bug into an achievement called "A Feature, Not A Bug" — and means it. That is the exact tone of the **It's Fine demo**, a free Steam demo of an automation and management game where you build bot farms, print legendary artifacts, jump between timelines, and slowly realize the whole operation is held together with duct tape and vibes. The It's Fine demo is also far more generous than most demos: a full tutorial arc, Overdrive challenges, a Dreamtree progression system, and 67 achievements to chase.

Why does that matter? Because achievements are the real endgame here. According to the community-maintained [It's Fine Wiki achievements page](https://itsfine.wiki.gg/wiki/Achievements), every single achievement is **unmissable** — you can earn all of them at any point after finishing the game. Nothing is locked behind a one-time event, so you can play your first run relaxed and clean up afterward.

That design choice makes the demo a low-risk entry point for completionists who hate missable content. Play badly, play slowly, restart a timeline — it does not matter. The list will still be waiting for you at the end.

## How Many Achievements Does the It's Fine Demo Have?

The It's Fine demo ships with 67 Steam achievements split into three tiers. Community reports recorded this list during the v0.2.136-Steam_Demo build, so a future update could shift the numbers slightly.

| Tier | Count | How It Unlocks | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 52 | Story progress and specific player actions | Visible from the start |
| Hidden | 14 | Odd, joke-driven actions | Designed as developer in-jokes |
| Secret | 1 | Completing the game | Hidden until you beat it |
| **Total** | **67** | — | All are unmissable |

The most important structural detail is this: hidden achievements do not appear on your achievements page until you finish the game. Your first playthrough will show a suspiciously short list, and the full picture only opens up after the credits roll.

### What "Unmissable" Actually Means

In practice, unmissable means you should never restart a run out of achievement anxiety. Nothing expires, nothing is tied to a single dialogue choice, and nothing requires a specific difficulty. Whether you rush the main objective or spend twenty hours optimizing your bot farm, the cleanup phase is always available.

That said, a few achievements are much easier to grab *during* normal play than to grind for later. "Hanging with my buds," for example, just asks you to leave 10 Minibots unassigned at once — trivial early on, mildly annoying once your whole factory depends on them.

## Hidden and Secret Achievements: The Best Jokes in the It's Fine Demo

The hidden achievements exist mostly because the developers wanted to hide jokes in the game. You will not see them listed until you beat the story, which is why their reveal is one of the more satisfying moments for completionists.

Two achievements gate the secret content. "A Feature, Not A Bug" unlocks the first time you find any secret, and roughly 40.8% of players have it — a surprisingly high number given how easy it is to walk past these. "YO LISTEN!" sits at just 2.6% and rewards the Joe of Flies skin for finding every single one.

Community reports also suggest the secret ending requires some secret achievements to be completed first, so if you are hunting the true finale, the joke achievements are not optional.

## Tips to 100% the It's Fine Demo Faster

If your goal is a clean sweep, order of operations matters more than raw speed. Here is the approach that wastes the least time.

- **Finish the story first.** Since nothing is missable, the optimal route is to complete the game, unlock the full achievement list, then work backward.
- **Build the Dreamtree early.** Most of the rare achievements reference Dreamtree upgrades directly, so a strong tree makes everything else cheaper.
- **Bank Sleep Hours in one timeline.** "Sleeper Agent" wants 80 Sleep Hours without a timeline reset, so commit to a single long run.
- **Print buff duration on artifacts.** "Buffed" needs three different buffs active on the same station at once, and printed buff duration is the most reliable way to hold that window open.
- **Clear Overdrive sets in order of completion rate.** Coffee Machine, then Cloud Servers, then Kraken — momentum beats frustration.
- **Stockpile Minibots for the Kraken.** "Master Exploader" requires sacrificing 66 of them, so start hoarding before you need to.
- **Avoid certain upgrades if you chase "Bombardilo."** Community reports warn against buying the "Short Form Content" Dreamtree upgrade or the "Telesco-creep" room upgrade for that one.
- **Use Auto-Print for artifacts.** "Sack Overflow" wants every unique artifact, and manual printing turns that into a chore.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the It's Fine demo free on Steam?**
Yes. Steam demos are free to download and play, so you can chase all 67 achievements without spending anything. If you enjoy the automation loop, the demo is a fair sample of the full experience.

**How many achievements are in the It's Fine demo?**
There are 67 total: 52 standard, 14 hidden, and 1 secret. The hidden ones stay invisible on your achievements page until you beat the game.

**Can you miss any achievements in the It's Fine demo?**
No. According to community documentation, every achievement is unmissable and can be earned at any point after completing the game, which is why most guides recommend finishing the story before you start optimizing.

**Why are some achievements hidden in the It's Fine demo?**
They function as developer in-jokes. The team deliberately tucked odd, secret-triggered achievements behind the end of the game so the reveal lands as a reward rather than a checklist item.
