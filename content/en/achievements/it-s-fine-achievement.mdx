## Why the It's Fine Achievement List Is Worth Your Time

With 67 achievements packed into a single Steam demo, It's Fine turns a laid-back automation game into a surprisingly long checklist. If you are hunting every **It's Fine achievement**, the good news is that none of them can be permanently missed — each one stays obtainable even after the credits roll. The catch is that several demand such specific setups that most players never bother.

That is exactly why a dedicated **It's Fine achievement** guide saves time: it separates what happens on its own from what needs planning. According to the [community-maintained It's Fine wiki](https://itsfine.wiki.gg/wiki/Achievements), the current list breaks down into 52 standard achievements, 14 hidden ones, and a single secret achievement, documented against the v0.2.136 Steam demo build. Steam's global completion percentages tell an even clearer story — most players finish the tutorial, and only a small fraction ever reach 100%.

Below you will find every tier of the list, the achievements worth targeting first, the ones that punish sloppy planning, and a roadmap for a clean completion run.

## How Many Achievements Does It's Fine Have?

The count is stable across the demo build, though it can shift with future updates. Here is the official breakdown as reported by the wiki.

| Category | Count | How It Appears In-Game |
|---|---|---|
| Standard achievements | 52 | Visible on the achievements page from the start |
| Hidden achievements | 14 | Not listed until unlocked; described by the wiki as a developer joke |
| Secret achievement | 1 | Hidden from the achievements page until you beat the game |
| **Total** | **67** | |

Some of these unlock automatically as the story progresses. Others require you to take a specific action, buy a particular upgrade, or deliberately avoid doing something. The good news for completionists: because nothing is missable, you can clean up the entire list after finishing the game rather than restarting.

### Hidden vs. secret achievements — what's the difference?

This trips people up constantly. Hidden achievements are the developers' punchline — they exist as in-jokes and only appear on your list once you have already earned them. The single secret achievement behaves differently: it stays invisible until you complete the main game.

Community reports also suggest that unlocking the game's secret ending requires having certain secret achievements completed first, so the hidden layer is not purely cosmetic.

## Tips for a Smooth It's Fine Achievement Run

A little structure turns a 67-entry list into a manageable project. Here is what experienced players recommend.

- **Finish the story first.** Since nothing is missable, the main game is the best tutorial you will get. You will unlock roughly half the list without trying.
- **Treat Overdrive challenges as one project.** Six achievements map to clearing full Overdrive sets — Cloud Servers, the Kraken, Peanut's reactor, Robotics Bay, the Ancient 3D Printer, and the Garden — plus Challenge Accepted for beating them all.
- **Read your Dreamtree before spending.** Gardeneer, Lost in Translation, and Real Civil all depend on specific Giga upgrades, and rerouting mid-run wastes timelines.
- **Print buff duration on artifacts early.** It is the quiet enabler behind Buffed and several sleep-based unlocks.
- **Schedule your no-purchase run.** Passive-Aggressive sits at 5.2% purely because players buy something reflexively. Decide in advance that one run is a spending freeze.
- **Use Auto-Print for artifact hunting.** Sack Overflow becomes a waiting game rather than a clicking game.
- **Do not buy "Short Form Content" or the "Telesco-creep" room upgrade if you want Bombardilo.** That achievement needs a lightning strike on the Botfarm while every phone is golden, and those two purchases get in the way.

## FAQ

**How many achievements does It's Fine have?**
The current count is 67: 52 standard, 14 hidden, and one secret achievement. The hidden entries function as a developer joke and only appear once you have earned them, while the secret achievement stays invisible until you beat the game.

**Are any It's Fine achievements missable?**
No. Community reports and the wiki agree that every achievement can be earned at any point after finishing the game, which means you never need to restart a save file for a missed unlock. That said, a few — like Passive-Aggressive — require you to avoid certain purchases for an entire run, so planning still matters.

**What is the difference between hidden and secret achievements?**
Hidden achievements are unlisted jokes that reveal themselves when unlocked. The secret achievement is withheld from the achievements page until you complete the main story, and it appears to be tied to accessing the game's secret ending.

**What is the rarest It's Fine achievement?**
YO LISTEN!, which requires finding every secret in the game, sits at roughly 2.6% on Steam. It also rewards the Joe of Flies skin. Master Exploader (4.4%) and Passive-Aggressive (5.2%) round out the three hardest unlocks in the list.

Whether you are chasing a single stubborn unlock or the full 100%, the It's Fine achievement list rewards patience far more than speed. Pick your phases, protect your no-purchase run, and the rarest entries will fall in time.
