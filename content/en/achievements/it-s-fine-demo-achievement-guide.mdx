If you launched the Steam demo expecting a relaxing idle game and instead found a 67-icon checklist staring back at you, you're not alone. This It's Fine demo achievement guide walks through every unlockable in the current build — which ones pop automatically, which demand deliberate setup, and which only the most stubborn players ever finish. Think of it as the It's Fine demo achievement guide that should have shipped with the game.

Why does it matter? Because a handful of these achievements are pure patience tests, and several sit under 5% completion on Steam. Knowing what order to chase them in — and which upgrades actively sabotage your progress — can save you hours of wasted timeline jumps.

## How the It's Fine Achievement System Works

The demo build tracked by the community wiki contains 67 achievements split across three visibility tiers. Understanding the tiers matters more than it sounds, because hidden achievements are deliberately written as developer jokes, and the secret entry stays invisible on your achievements page until you beat the game.

| Tier | Count | Visible Upfront? | What It Contains |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | 52 | Yes | Story progress and action-based unlocks |
| Hidden | 14 | No | Joke entries written by the developers |
| Secret | 1 | No | Revealed only after finishing the game |
| **Total** | **67** | — | Full demo checklist |

Every entry in this It's Fine demo achievement guide is, according to the community-maintained [It's Fine Wiki achievement list](https://itsfine.wiki.gg/wiki/Achievements), currently unmissable. That is the single best piece of news here: none of them require a fresh save file, and all of them can still be earned after you roll credits. If you finish the story and only have 30% completion, nothing is permanently lost.

One quirk worth flagging: finding any single secret grants **A Feature, Not A Bug** (roughly 40.8% of players), and secrets also gate access to the secret ending. So if you want the true finale, secret hunting isn't optional.

The top of the completion list is basically a tutorial reward track. These unlock through ordinary play, and if you're missing any of them, you likely stopped early rather than did something wrong.

**Hanging with my buds** is the easiest "missable-feeling" achievement in the game. You simply leave ten Minibots unassigned at the same time — no upgrades required. Players who reflexively assign every bot they build tend to overlook it for hours.

## Overdrive Challenges, Ranked by Difficulty

Overdrive challenges are the backbone of the demo's mid-game, and there is one achievement per station plus a capstone for clearing them all. The spread below shows how sharply difficulty climbs.

Notice the cliff between Cloud Servers at 56% and the Garden at 20.6%. The Garden challenges demand that your Dreamtree is already developed, which is why so many players stall there. According to community reports, the practical move is to bank Dreamtree upgrades before touching Garden Overdrive at all — going in underprepared just burns a run.

**Challenge Accepted** (beat every Overdrive challenge) sits at roughly 20.1%, meaning about four out of five players never finish the whole set. Treat it as your long-term goal rather than a session task.

## The Mid-Tier Grind: Megabots, Cats, and Artifacts

Once the freebies are gone, the middle of the list is where a good It's Fine demo achievement guide earns its keep. These are all solvable — they just need specific purchases lined up in the right order.

| Achievement | What You Need | Completion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full Stack | Install all 6 Cloud Servers | ~38.5% |
| Legend Has It | Print a Legendary artifact | ~36.8% |
| Go Big or Go Bot | Buy a Megabot | ~36.0% |

| Lost in Translation | Highest-rarity Localization bonus | ~20.0% |

A few field notes from player experience:

- **The Clickers Clique** gets much easier once you upgrade Outsourcing in the Dreamtree and add Betty the Megabot — those two together push you past five active cursors without micromanaging.
- **Gardeneer** is almost free if you grab the Giga Garden upgrade, since the Strawhat Megabot then handles the full seed-to-harvest cycle alone.
- **Good Boy!** depends on Joe skins, several of which are themselves achievement rewards (Call Center Joe, Indiana Joe, Slime Joe, Champion Joe). If you haven't earned one yet, this stays locked.
- **Purrfect Alignment** is the fussiest of the group: Shiro, Jinx, the Catlady Megabot, and five active cat traits must all exist at the same moment in Schrödinger's Machine.

## The Hardest Achievements in the It's Fine Demo

These are the numbers that make an It's Fine demo achievement guide genuinely useful, because most players never see them.

| Achievement | Requirement | Completion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YO LISTEN! | Find every secret | ~2.6% |
| Master Exploader | Sacrifice 66 Minibots to the Kraken | ~4.4% |
| Passive-Aggressive | Sleep with 1+ Sleep Hour without buying anything all run | ~5.2% |
| Buffed | Garden, Headhunter, and Vandalist buffs on one station | ~14.7% |
| It's Fine | Finish the game | ~15.6% |
| Real Civil | Complete a Localization order for all 5 civilizations | ~17.5% |
| Sack Overflow | Collect every Unique artifact | ~16.7% |
| Toxic Positivity | Reach maximum Timeline Toxicity | ~16.6% |
| Bombardilo | Lightning strikes the Botfarm while every phone is golden | ~18.1% |

Practical advice for the nastiest ones:

- **Master Exploader** is a numbers game. Build Minibots in bulk and feed the Kraken until you hit 66. There is no shortcut, only volume.
- **Passive-Aggressive** requires a run where you buy *nothing* after jumping timelines. Sleep, bank the hours, pour them into GPU Rack upgrades in the Dreamtree, then wait. Any purchase voids the attempt.
- **Bombardilo** conflicts with common builds: you must avoid "Short Form Content" in the Dreamtree and skip the Telesco-creep room upgrade so your phones stay golden when lightning hits.
- **YO LISTEN!** rewards the Joe of Flies skin and demands every secret in the game. Given that the secret ending also requires secret achievements, this is naturally the last thing you'll finish.

## A Practical Route to 100% Completion

Because several achievements pull in opposite directions — Passive-Aggressive forbids purchases, while Peak Interior and Gotta Bot 'Em All require them — plan on multiple runs or timelines rather than one perfect playthrough.

| Order | Focus | Why It Comes Here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Finish the story | Reveals secret achievements and unlocks endgame systems |
| 2 | Clear every Overdrive challenge | Feeds Challenge Accepted plus 7 station achievements |
| 3 | Buy out rooms, servers, and Megabots | Peak Interior, Full Stack, Gotta Bot 'Em All |
| 4 | Bank Dreamtree upgrades | Prerequisite for most action-based unlocks |
| 5 | Auto-Print artifacts | Sack Overflow and Legend Has It become nearly automatic |
| 6 | Hunt secrets and run the no-purchase timeline | A Feature, Not A Bug, YO LISTEN!, Passive-Aggressive |

Two efficiency tips worth repeating: the game rewards patience over speed, and nothing here is permanently missable. If an attempt fails, jump timelines and try again — the wiki's data shows most players eventually get there.

## FAQ

**How many achievements does the It's Fine demo have?**
The current demo build contains 67 achievements: 52 normal, 14 hidden developer jokes, and 1 secret achievement that stays hidden until you beat the game.

**Can you permanently miss achievements in It's Fine?**
No. Per the community wiki, every achievement is currently unmissable and can be earned at any point — including after completing the story — so a failed attempt never costs you the unlock.

**What is the rarest achievement in the demo?**
Based on Steam completion data, **YO LISTEN!** (find every secret) is the rarest at roughly 2.6%, followed by **Master Exploader** at about 4.4% and **Passive-Aggressive** at about 5.2%.

**Does this It's Fine demo achievement guide apply to the full release?**
It reflects the Steam demo version tracked by the community wiki. Achievement names, counts, and requirements can shift as the game updates, so verify anything time-sensitive against the live in-game list.
